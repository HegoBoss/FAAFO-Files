Sprite

Sprite is a lemon-lime flavored carbonated soft drink. It is a popular choice for those who enjoy a refreshing, citrusy beverage. Sprite is known for its clear appearance and crisp taste, making it a favorite among many consumers.

Ingredients:

Carbonated water, high fructose corn syrup, citric acid, natural flavors, sodium benzoate (preservative), artificial colors (Yellow 6, Red 40), caffeine.

Nutritional Information (per 12 fl oz/355 ml):

  • Calories: 150
  • Total Fat: 0g
  • Sodium: 45mg
  • Total Carbohydrates: 41g
  • Sugars: 41g
  • Protein: 0g
  • Caffeine: 34mg

Allergen Information:

Sprite does not contain any major allergens. However, it is always advisable to check the product label for the most up-to-date information.
