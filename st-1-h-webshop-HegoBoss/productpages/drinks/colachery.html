Coca-Cola Cherry

Coca-Cola Cherry is a cherry-flavored version of the classic Coca-Cola soda. It combines the familiar taste of Coca-Cola with a sweet cherry flavor, creating a unique and refreshing beverage option for those who enjoy fruity twists on traditional sodas. Coca-Cola Cherry is often enjoyed on its own or as a mixer in various cocktails.

Ingredients:

Carbonated water, high fructose corn syrup, caramel color, phosphoric acid, natural flavors, caffeine, citric acid, cherry juice from concentrate.

Nutritional Information (per 12 fl oz/355 ml):

  • Calories: 150
  • Total Fat: 0g
  • Sodium: 45mg
  • Total Carbohydrates: 41g
  • Sugars: 41g
  • Protein: 0g
  • Caffeine: 34mg

Allergen Information:

Coca-Cola Cherry does not contain any major allergens. However, it is always advisable to check the product label for the most up-to-date information.
