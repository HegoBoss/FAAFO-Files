Coca-Cola

Coca-Cola is a carbonated soft drink that was created in 1886 by pharmacist John Pemberton in Atlanta, Georgia. It is one of the most popular and recognizable brands in the world, known for its distinctive red and white logo and its unique flavor profile.

Ingredients:

Carbonated water, high fructose corn syrup, caramel color, phosphoric acid, natural flavors, caffeine, citric acid.

Nutritional Information (per 12 fl oz/355 ml):

  • Calories: 150
  • Total Fat: 0g
  • Sodium: 45mg
  • Total Carbohydrates: 41g
  • Sugars: 41g
  • Protein: 0g
  • Caffeine: 34mg

Allergen Information:

Coca-Cola does not contain any major allergens. However, it is always advisable to check the product label for the most up-to-date information.
