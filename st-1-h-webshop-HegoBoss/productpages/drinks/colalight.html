Coca-Cola Light

Coca-Cola Light is a reduced-calorie version of the classic Coca-Cola soda. It combines the familiar taste of Coca-Cola with a sweetener to provide a refreshing beverage option for those who enjoy the taste of Coca-Cola but are looking to reduce their calorie intake. Coca-Cola Light is often enjoyed on its own or as a mixer in various cocktails.

Ingredients:

Carbonated Water, Caramel Color, Phosphoric Acid, Aspartame, Caffeine, Natural Flavors, Citric Acid, Acesulfame Potassium.

Nutritional Information (per 12 fl oz/355 ml):

  • Calories: 150
  • Total Fat: 0g
  • Sodium: 45mg
  • Total Carbohydrates: 41g
  • Sugars: 0g
  • Protein: 0g
  • Caffeine: 34mg

Allergen Information:

Coca-Cola Light does not contain any major allergens. However, it is always advisable to check the product label for the most up-to-date information.
