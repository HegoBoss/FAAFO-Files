Iets Speciaal

Iets Speciaal is a buttery, flaky, and crescent-shaped pastry made from laminated dough. It's a popular breakfast item in many countries and is often served with butter or jam. The pastry is known for its distinctive shape and the layers of buttery, flaky texture that result from the lamination process.

Ingredients:

Flour, butter, milk, sugar, salt, yeast.
